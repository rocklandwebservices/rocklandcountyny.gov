Ballot Propositions

A ballot proposition (also referred to as a ballot measure, initiative, or referendum) is a proposed law, constitutional amendment, or public policy question placed directly before voters to decide on election day.

Unlike voting for candidates to represent you, voting on a proposition lets you vote directly on the policy itself.

On June 16, 2026, a local law amending the Rockland County Charter was unanimously passed by the County Legislature and then signed by the County Executive on July 2, 2026, a change supported by both branches of government. This will be presented to voters on the Tuesday, November 3, 2026, General Election Ballot.
